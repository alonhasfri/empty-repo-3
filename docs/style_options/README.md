# Style options: the same street in 6 looks

These are quick mock-ups made with simple 3D boxes, to compare **art styles**, not layouts. Every style shows the same street: shops, houses, a plaza with a fountain, and the Brain Tower. `ALL_STYLES.png` shows all six side by side. Each style also has a street-level picture (`styleN_street.png`) and a high-angle picture (`styleN_high.png`).

1. **Cute & polished (inspired by Adopt Me's feel):** soft pastel colours, rounded chunky shapes and lots of cute decor (bunting, flowers, round lamps). It's friendly and very "Roblox hit game". It runs well on phones and is easy to build in Roblox with smooth parts and simple materials. The shapes and assets are our own.
2. **Semi-realistic textured:** real brick, plaster, asphalt and paving textures with natural colours. It feels like a polished life-sim town. It's medium on phones and needs MaterialVariants or SurfaceAppearance textures (medium effort).
3. **Realistic city:** tall concrete and glass buildings, a grey palette and grittier roads. It feels like a real big city. It's the heaviest on phones (big buildings and textures), and the hardest to make look good, since it needs good meshes.
4. **Toon / cel-shaded:** flat bright colours with black outlines, like a comic or cartoon. It's light on phones. Outlines in Roblox need the Highlight object or outline meshes (medium effort), and it looks very unique.
5. **Cozy storybook:** golden-hour light, cobblestone streets, timber and brick shops with steep red roofs and flower boxes. It's warm and magical. It's medium on phones, and the timber and roof textures plus the warm lighting are easy to do in Roblox.
6. **Clean low-poly:** minimal flat colours and simple shapes. It's the lightest on phones and the easiest to build, and it's also what a **Low detail mode** could look like for any of the other styles.

Tip: you can mix styles, for example "2, but with the colours and decor of 1".
