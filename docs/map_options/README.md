# Map options: pick your town

Five different town layouts, all in the blocky Roblox style. They use brighter, natural colours, real roads with sidewalks and streetlights, house neighbourhoods, and special buildings you can go into. Each option has three pictures:
- **aerial**: the whole town from above at an angle
- **street**: what a player sees standing in the street
- **topdown**: a labelled plan. Pink labels are Lose-side places, blue labels are Gain-side places.

`ALL_OPTIONS.png` shows all five side by side. `interior_before_after.png` shows how the insides of buildings could look: warm, real-sized and full of things, instead of grey and empty.

These are mock-ups made from simple 3D boxes to compare ideas. They aren't the real game map yet.

## 1. Main Street Town
One long main street through the plaza. Loud shops are on one end and the smart buildings on the other, and curvy side streets lead to cosy house neighbourhoods and cul-de-sacs.
- **Good:** easy to understand and hard to get lost in, it feels like a real small town, and Lose vs Gain is clear (left vs right).
- **Watch out:** the ends of the main street are far apart, so a bus or taxi along Main Street makes sense. Lots of houses, but they're only decoration.

## 2. River City
A river splits the town. The **Gain side** is a downtown grid with tall buildings. The **Lose side** is a loud boardwalk with stalls and neon shops. Three bridges connect them, with suburbs behind.
- **Good:** the river makes the two paths feel like two different worlds, bridges are natural places to meet, and boats are fun.
- **Watch out:** crossing means using a bridge, so place bridges near the plaza. It's a little more complex to build.

## 3. Downtown + Suburb Ring
A busy downtown grid around the plaza. The Lose shops are on the west half and the Gain buildings on the east. A green park belt circles it, then a ring road with suburbs and round neighbourhoods.
- **Good:** looks the most like a real city, with tall buildings in the middle and houses outside. Every special building is in the centre, close together, which is great on phones.
- **Watch out:** a grid can look the same everywhere, so each block needs its own colour or sign. The ring road is long, so a taxi or bus suits it well.

## 4. Hill Town
The town climbs a hill in steps. The loud **Lose market is at the bottom** and the **Brain Tower, University and smart buildings are at the top**, so getting smarter literally means going up. Winding roads and stairs connect the levels.
- **Good:** the strongest "story" of the five (smart = up, dumb = down), with great views from the top. Stairs are a fun shortcut.
- **Watch out:** walking uphill is slow, so the taxi and bus really matter. Stairs and ramps need careful testing on phones, and it's the hardest one to build.

## 5. Island Town
A round island in the sea. A beach and harbour with boats are on one side, and a **Lose boardwalk with an arcade** and a lighthouse on the beach. A **Gain cliff-top** has the Observatory, University and Science Lab. Houses fill the middle.
- **Good:** the most "Roblox-y" and memorable, with natural edges (the sea), so players never walk off the map. Easy to make a great thumbnail.
- **Watch out:** space is limited, so it can feel crowded. The cliff needs a ramp, and the sea is a lot of empty blue on a phone screen.

## Mixing is allowed
You can combine ideas, e.g. "Option 3 with the river from 2" or "Option 1 but on a hill".
