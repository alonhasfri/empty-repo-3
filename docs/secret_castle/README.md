# The Secret Castle Path

These are concept pictures for the hidden admin secret chain (`ALL_STAGES.png` shows the whole path). They're three.js mock-ups, not Roblox screenshots.

| # | Stage | How to get to the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Konami Code** (`stage1.png`) | Anywhere in town, type ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← →: arrow keys or WASD on PC, the D-pad on controller, or 8 quick swipes on phones. You teleport to the Admin House. |
| 2 | **The Admin House** (`stage2.png`) | A secret room with a trophy wall, a dev photo board and a desk with too many monitors. Walk to the hole in the floor at the back (the sign says "don't jump 👀") and jump in. |
| 3 | **Fall into the Void** (`stage3.png`) | You fall through starry black space and land somewhere very white. |
| 4 | **The White Room** (`stage4.png`) | An endless white space with one cardboard box labelled "please $1". Give it **1 coin of game money** (never real money) to start the obby. |
| 5 | **The White Obby** (`stage5.png`) | Jump across the floating platforms, dodge the moving blocks and the spinning bar, use the checkpoint flags, and reach the castle gate. |
| 6 | **The Admin Castle** (`stage6.png`) | A giant empty stone hall with only one bed and one lamp. Stay in the room for **5 minutes** and you're pulled into the Infinite Castle. |
| 7 | **The Infinite Castle** (`stage7.png`) | Identical rooms loop forever, with doors on all 4 sides. Walk through doors in the code: **2 rooms forward, 2 rooms back, left, right, left, right**. Any other route just loops. |
| 8 | **The Secret Admin Room** (`stage8.png`) | You did it! Take the **Castle Map** (teleport anywhere) and earn the **"Where am I?"** badge. |

Rules: kid-safe (creepy-cozy at most), no real money anywhere, and non-admins get no admin powers in the Admin House (the server still checks staff rank).
